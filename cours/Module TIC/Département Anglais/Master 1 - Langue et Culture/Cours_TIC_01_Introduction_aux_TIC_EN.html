Learning Objectives

Knowledge: - Analyze ICT as a research object in language didactics - Know research methods on digital uses - Master documentary research and analysis tools

Skills: - Design a research framework integrating digital tools - Analyze digital corpora (forums, online interactions) - Use data processing tools (NVivo, Python for text analysis)

Attitudes: - Adopt a reflective posture on one's own digital use - Contribute to digital didactics research

1. Definition and Evolution (10 min)

ICT designates all digital technologies for processing and transmitting information.

Recent evolution: - Web 2.0 (social media, collaboration) - Generative AI (ChatGPT, 2022+) - Learning Analytics and big data - Virtual and augmented reality in education

Master-level issue: ICT are not just tools — they transform teaching/learning processes and constitute a research field in themselves.

2. ICT as a Research Object (20 min)

Research axes in language didactics:

Axis Research question
E-learning Is online learning as effective as face-to-face?
NLP/AI Can AI replace teachers for correction?
Corpora How do digital corpora improve learning?
Social media Do social networks foster acquisition?
Motivation Does gamification improve motivation?

Key references: Warschauer (1996), Chapelle (2003), Kukulska-Hulme (2012).

3. Research Methodologies (20 min)

Approaches: - Quantitative: experiments, standardized tests, statistics - Qualitative: interviews, observations, discourse analysis - Mixed: data triangulation

Methodological tools: - Online questionnaire (Google Forms, LimeSurvey) - Trace analysis (Learning Analytics) - Semi-structured interviews via videoconference - Textual corpus analysis (AntConc, TXM)

4. Digital Tools for Research (15 min)

Tool Function
Zotero / EndNote Bibliographic management
NVivo / MAXQDA Qualitative analysis
SPSS / R Statistical analysis
AntConc / TXM Corpus analysis
Google Scholar / ERIC Documentary research
Overleaf / LaTeX Academic writing

5. Digital Corpora (15 min)

Corpus types: - Learner language forums - Written productions on platforms - Virtual classroom interactions - Audio/video recordings

Process: collection → transcription → annotation → analysis.

6. Research Ethics (10 min)

  • Informed consent from participants
  • Data anonymization
  • GDPR and data protection
  • Citation of corpora used

7. Monitoring and Publishing (15 min)

Monitoring: Google Alerts, ResearchGate, Academia.edu Key journals in digital didactics: - ALSIC, CALL, ReCALL, Language Learning & Technology

8. Conclusion (5 min)

  • ICT are an essential research field in didactics
  • Varied methodologies (quantitative, qualitative, mixed)
  • Accessible tools for beginning researchers
