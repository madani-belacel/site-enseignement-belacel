Exercise 1 — Personal definition (15 min)

Statement: Give your own definition of ICT in 2-3 lines, then compare it to the ITU definition.

Detailed correction: ICT brings together digital tools for processing, transmission and access to information. The ITU emphasizes digital form and the convergence of technologies.

Exercise 2 — The three dimensions (15 min)

Statement: Explain with personal examples the three dimensions: Techniques, Information, Communication.

Detailed correction: Technical = PC + Moodle; Information = PDF handout; Communication = email to teacher or TD forum.

Exercise 3 — ICT functions (18 min)

Statement: For each action, identify the function: processing / transmission / backup. Word writing, email sending, USB copying, Teams videoconferencing, PDF printing.

Detailed correction: Word → processing; email and Teams → transmission; USB → backup; printing → processing/output.

Exercise 4 — Language applications (18 min)

Statement: Name 5 concrete uses of ICT for a foreign language student.

Detailed correction: Online multilingual corpora, video with native speakers, Moodle for homework, digital library, PowerPoint for presentations.

Exercise 5 — Critical thinking (14 min)

Statement: Write a paragraph (10-12 lines) on the opportunities and risks of ICT for language students.

Detailed correction: Opportunities: global resources, MOOCs, collaboration. Risks: plagiarism, misinformation, addiction, privacy. Posture: critical thinking and academic ethics.
