Exercise 1 — Search engine (15 min)

Statement: Explain the role of crawlers and the benefit of a metasearch engine.

Detailed correction: - Crawlers: robots that explore the Web and index pages. - Metasearch engine: queries several engines, merges and deduplicates the results (e.g. Qwant, Dogpile).

Exercise 2 — Professional email (18 min)

Statement: Write an email to a teacher to request a TD postponement (subject, formula, structure).

Detailed correction: - Subject: [L1 Languages] TD TIC report request — Last name First name - Formula: "Madam, Sir," - Body: brief reason, desired date, politeness. - Signature: name, group, institutional email. - Attachment only if supporting documentation requested.

Exercise 3 — Email protocols (15 min)

Statement: Associate SMTP, POP, IMAP with: send, receive locally, multi-device sync.

Detailed correction: - SMTP → sending. - POP → reception (often local download). - IMAP → reception with box sync on multiple devices (mobile + PC recommended).

Exercise 4 — File transfer (18 min)

Statement: Compare USB key, Google Drive and email to submit a 15 MB assignment.

Detailed correction: - USB key: fast in person, risk of loss/virus. - Drive: sharing link, traceability, institutional quota limit. - Email: attachment limit (~25 MB depending on server), practical for small files. → For 15 MB: Drive or Moodle platform preferred.
