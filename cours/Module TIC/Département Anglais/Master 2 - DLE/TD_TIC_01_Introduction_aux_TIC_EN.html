📖 ICT Tutorial 01 — Introduction to Information and Communication Techniques

Dr. BELACEL Madani Maître de Conférences B (MCB)

Université de Mostaganem — Faculté des langues étrangères · Département d'anglais
📧 madani.belacel [at] univ-mosta.dz

Exercise 1 — Definition and theoretical framework (15 min)

Statement: Give your own definition of ICT in 2-3 lines, then compare it with the ITU, UNESCO, and digital literacy framework definitions.

Detailed correction: The ITU emphasizes digital convergence; UNESCO places ICT within 21st-century skills; digital literacy adds critical, ethical, and citizenship dimensions.

Exercise 2 — Three dimensions in research context (15 min)

Statement: For each dimension (Technical, Information, Communication), suggest an ICT tool used in language teaching research and justify your choice.

Detailed correction: Technical = corpus analysis software (AntConc); Information = ERIC/Google Scholar database; Communication = Zoom conference with automatic transcription.

Exercise 3 — Taxonomy of ICT functions (18 min)

Statement: Classify the following actions by function (processing/transmission/backup) AND by Bloom's taxonomy (create, evaluate, analyze, apply, understand, remember): collaborative writing of an article via Google Docs, journal submission via OJS, archiving on HAL, monitoring via Google Scholar alerts.

Detailed correction: Google Docs → processing + create; OJS → transmission + evaluate; HAL → backup + understand; alerts → transmission + analyze.

Exercise 4 — ICT and language research (18 min)

Statement: Design a research setup using 3 ICT tools for a study on vocabulary acquisition in EFL. Justify your tool choices.

Detailed correction: Online questionnaire (Google Forms) for pre-test; interactive exercise platform (LearningApps) for intervention; statistical analysis (Excel/SPSS) for post-test. Justification: free, accessible, adapted to the Algerian university context.

Exercise 5 — Critical synthesis and monitoring (14 min)

Statement: Write a synthesis note (15-20 lines) on the evolution of ICT in Algerian higher education since 2020, citing at least 2 academic references.

Detailed correction: Post-COVID acceleration (Moodle platforms, videoconferencing), access disparities, ministerial initiatives (CEM, ENT). References: MESRS 2022 reports, article on e-pedagogy in Algeria.

Exercise 6 — Critical article analysis (10 min) — Master level

Statement: Read the abstract of an article provided by the instructor on the impact of ICT in higher education. Identify: research question, method, main results, limitations.

Detailed correction: Depends on the article provided. Analysis grid: research question → 2 pts, methodology → 2 pts, results → 3 pts, limitations → 3 pts.
