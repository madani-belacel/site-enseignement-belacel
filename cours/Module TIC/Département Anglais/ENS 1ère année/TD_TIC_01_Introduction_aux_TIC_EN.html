ICT Tutorial 01 — Introduction to Information and Communication Techniques

Instructor: BELACEL Madani Module: ICT Level: ENS Year 1 — English Department Duration: 1h30 Academic year: 2025-2026

(Email and detailed progress of the session: see PDF cover page.)

Exercise 1 — Personal definition and transposition (15 min)

Statement: Give your own definition of ICT in 2-3 lines, then compare it to the ITU definition. As a future teacher, rephrase this definition for middle school students.

Detailed correction: ICT brings together digital tools for processing, transmission and access to information. Middle school version: "ICT is all the digital tools that help us create, share, and find information."

Exercise 2 — Three dimensions and pedagogy (15 min)

Statement: Explain with personal examples the three dimensions: Technical, Information, Communication. Then propose a teaching scenario where all three dimensions are used simultaneously.

Detailed correction: Example: a media project — Technical (creating a blog with WordPress), Information (researching articles), Communication (publishing and commenting). Students learn both the technical tool and critical thinking.

Exercise 3 — ICT functions in the classroom (18 min)

Statement: For each action, identify the function: processing / transmission / backup. Then for each action, propose an equivalent exercise adapted for a high school class.

Detailed correction: Word writing → processing (high school: write a cover letter); email sending → transmission (homework submission with attachment); USB copy → backup (organize files by subject).

Exercise 4 — ICT for language teachers (18 min)

Statement: Name 5 concrete uses of ICT for a foreign language teacher (not for the student).

Detailed correction: Moodle for course materials and assignments; Padlet for collaborative wall; Quizlet for flashcards; YouTube for video capsules; ENT for grade tracking and parent communication.

Exercise 5 — Lesson plan design (14 min)

Statement: Design a 55-minute lesson plan on "Introduction to ICT" for 10th grade. Objectives, procedure, ICT tools, assessment.

Detailed correction: Objective: distinguish information from communication. Procedure: 10 min debate, 25 min practical exercises (browsing, email), 15 min report writing, 5 min wrap-up. Tools: browser, email, word processor. Assessment: completed worksheet.

Exercise 6 — Digital citizenship (10 min)

Statement: Write an argumented response to a parent who opposes the use of digital tools in class (screen time, distraction, inequality).

Detailed correction: Arguments: ICT is part of the official curriculum; controlled and purposeful use (limited time, clear objectives); digital tools reduce inequality (free resources, automated feedback); the school's role is to train responsible digital citizens.
