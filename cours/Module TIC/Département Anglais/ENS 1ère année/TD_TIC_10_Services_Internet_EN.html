📖 ICT Tutorial 10 — Internet Services for Teaching

Dr. BELACEL Madani Maître de Conférences B (MCB)

Université de Mostaganem — Faculté des langues étrangères · Département d'anglais
madani.belacel [at] univ-mosta.dz

Exercise 1 — Search engines in the classroom (15 min)

Statement: Explain the role of crawlers and the benefit of a metasearch engine. Propose a classroom activity where students compare results from 3 search engines (Google, Qwant, DuckDuckGo) on the same topic.

Detailed correction: Crawlers: robots that index the Web. Metasearch: queries multiple engines (Qwant). Activity: students search "climate change" on 3 engines, compare results (number, relevance, ads), reflect on algorithmic bias.

Exercise 2 — Professional email for teachers (18 min)

Statement: Write an email to a parent to inform them of their child's difficulties. Subject, tone, structure, institutional signature. Then write an email to administration requesting a computer lab.

Detailed correction: Parent email: clear subject ([School X] Academic follow-up — First name Last name), empathetic tone, facts + suggestion + meeting proposal. Admin email: precise subject, pedagogical justification, requested date, necessary equipment.

Exercise 3 — Email protocols and school IT (15 min)

Statement: Associate SMTP, POP, IMAP with: send, receive locally, multi-device sync. Explain why IMAP is recommended for teachers using school PC + phone + tablet.

Detailed correction: SMTP → sending; POP → local download (not recommended, desync); IMAP → cloud sync (recommended for mobile teachers). With IMAP, a draft started on the phone is finished on the PC.

Exercise 4 — Learning platforms (18 min)

Statement: Compare Moodle, Google Classroom and Padlet on 5 criteria (free, file upload, quizzes, grading, communication). Determine which is most suitable for Algerian middle schools.

Detailed correction: Moodle: complete, free, but heavy setup. Google Classroom: simple, intuitive, but requires Google accounts. Padlet: visual collaborative wall but limited. For Algerian middle schools: Google Classroom (simplicity, availability, light infrastructure). Moodle for high school and university.

Exercise 5 — Pedagogical scenario with the Internet (14 min)

Statement: Design a 2-session sequence (2h) on "Searching and evaluating online information" for 9th grade. Objectives, procedure, tools, assessment.

Detailed correction: Session 1: guided search (keywords, operators, reliable/unreliable sources). Session 2: critical evaluation (website analysis grid). Tools: browser, word processor, collaborative board. Assessment: source evaluation sheet for a presentation.

Exercise 6 — Cyberbullying and digital citizenship (10 min)

Statement: Write an argumented response to a student who posts photos of classmates on social media without permission. What does the law say? What pedagogical response?

Detailed correction: Legal framework: right to image, GDPR for personal data. Pedagogical response: school rules reminder, awareness of consequences (disciplinary sanctions, criminal complaint), empathy work. Proposal: have the class write a social media usage charter.
