Course Plan

  1. Importance of Microsoft Word in the university career
  2. Word interface and main areas
  3. Academic layout (university standards)
  4. Professional use of Styles
  5. Automatic table of contents
  6. Tables, lists and visual elements
  7. Saving, PDF export and best practices
  8. Conclusion and transition to Excel

1. Why master Word? (10 mins)

Word allows you to produce homework, reading sheets, reports and written presentations. A well-formatted document reflects the seriousness of the student.

2. Interface (15 min)

Ribbon (Home, Insert, Layout, References), page area, navigation pane, status bar.

3. Academic Layout (20 min)

Times New Roman or Calibri 12 font, 1.5 line spacing, 2.5 cm margins, justified text, pagination, headers/footers.

4. Styles (20 min)

Heading 1, Heading 2, Heading 3, Normal — consistent formatting and automatic table of contents.

5. Table of Contents (10 min)

Apply styles Title → References → Table of Contents → Update.

6. Tables, Lists, Images (10 min)

Numbered lists, comparative tables, images with captions.

7. Recording and Export (5 min)

.docx (editable) + .pdf (final submission) + Ctrl+S frequent.

8. Conclusion (10 min)

Next lesson: Microsoft Excel — Basics and academic uses.
