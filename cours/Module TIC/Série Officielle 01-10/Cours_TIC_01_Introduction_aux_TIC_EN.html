1. Introduction and Importance of ICT (10 min)

Information and Communication Technologies (ICT) refer to all the digital tools that make it possible to create, process, store, transmit and share information. They include computers, smartphones, wireless networks and online platforms.

Why this course in License 1? From the first year, you use ICT to:

  • Access resources: Moodle, digital library, PDF handouts
  • Communicate: institutional email, TD forums
  • Search: documentary bases, multilingual linguistic corpora
  • Produce: Word homework, PowerPoint presentations, lectures

Educational objective: develop a solid digital culture — knowing how to use the tools, but also understanding their limits (reliability of sources, plagiarism, private life).

2. Detailed Definition of ICT (15 min)

Convergence of three domains

  1. Computer science — automatic processing of information (software, databases)
  2. Telecommunications — remote transmission (Internet, fiber, Wi-Fi)
  3. Audiovisual and multimedia — text, image, sound, video combined

Reference definitions

  • ITU: "ICT encompasses all the tools and systems that make it possible to manipulate, store and transmit information in digital form. »
  • UNESCO: IT, audiovisual, multimedia, Internet and telecommunications.
  • In English: Information and Communication Technologies (ICT).

To remember: an ICT is a system (hardware + software + network) that makes information usable and shareable.

3. Historical and Terminological Evolution (NICT → ICT) (10 min)

Period Term Meaning
1990–2005 NICT Emphasis on newness: Internet, email, GSM
Since ~2005 TIC / ICT Commonplace technologies, integrated into everyday life

Related concepts:

  • Digitalization: conversion into digital files (PDF, MP3, JPEG)
  • Digital transformation: reorganization of activities thanks to digital technology
  • Information society: information becomes a central resource

Example: course on Moodle + recorded video + PDF homework + online correction.

4. The Three Fundamental Dimensions (15 min)

  1. Technical dimension — hardware and software resources (PC, smartphone, Word, browser)
  2. Information dimension — manipulated content (text, image, sound, video; PDF, DOCX, MP4 formats)
  3. Communication dimension — exchange between people (email, forum, videoconference)

Mental exercise: for an assignment on Moodle — technique = PC + platform; information = Word file; communication = deposit visible to the teacher.

5. Role and Functions of ICT (15 min)

Function Description Examples
Processing Entry, formatting, calculation Word, Excel, image editing
Transmission Remote sending or sharing Email, Teams, Drive link
Backup Sustainable conservation USB key, cloud, university server

Complete scenario: Word writing → sending by email → copying to USB key and cloud. The three functions complement each other. 

flowchart LR
    A[Create document] --> B[Processing: Word]
    B --> C[Transmission: Email]
    C --> D[Backup: USB + Cloud]
    D --> E[Secure storage]

6. Main ICT Services (10 min)

  • Email (@univ-mosta.dz)
  • Web browsing and search
  • Videoconference (Teams, Zoom)
  • Learning platforms (Moodle, Google Classroom)
  • Online storage and sharing (Drive, OneDrive)

Distinction: a service (Moodle) relies on technologies (server, network) for an educational function.

7. Applications in Education (10 min)

  • E-learning and hybrid teaching
  • Digital libraries (24-hour access)
  • Remote collaborative work
  • Online assessment (MCQ, anti-plagiarism)
  • Multilingual documentary research

For language students: linguistic corpora, exchanges with native speakers, podcasts, monitoring of reliable sources in the target language.

Issue: multiplying resources requires critical thinking (check the source, cite correctly).

8. Expected Digital Skills (10 min)

  1. Academic office automation (Word, PowerPoint)
  2. Documentary research (keywords, credibility of sites)
  3. File management (naming, PDF/DOCX formats)
  4. Professional communication by email
  5. Security and ethics (passwords, plagiarism, privacy)

9. Conclusion (5 min)

  • ICT = IT, telecoms and multimedia convergence
  • Three dimensions: technique, information, communication
  • Three functions: processing, transmission, backup
  • Essential transversal skills

MCQ — Knowledge Check (5 questions)

Q1: What does ICT stand for? - A) Integrated Computer Technology - B) Information and Communication Techniques - C) Internet Communication Tools - D) International Computing Terminology

Answer: B

Q2: Which of the following is NOT one of the three fundamental dimensions of ICT? - A) Technical - B) Information - C) Financial - D) Communication

Answer: C

Q3: According to the ITU, ICT encompasses tools that manipulate, store and transmit information in which form? - A) Analog - B) Physical - C) Digital - D) Optical

Answer: C

Q4: Which term was used before "ICT" to emphasize new technologies in the 1990s? - A) DCT - B) NICT - C) PICT - D) MICT

Answer: B

Q5: Which function does saving a file to a USB key represent? - A) Processing - B) Transmission - C) Backup - D) Analysis

Answer: C
