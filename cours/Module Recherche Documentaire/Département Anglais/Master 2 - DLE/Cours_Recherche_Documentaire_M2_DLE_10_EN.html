Documentary Research Course — M2 DLE — Session 10 (EN)

Lecturer: BELACEL Madani
Module: Documentary Research — Master 2 DLE
Duration: 1h30
Academic Year: 2025-2026

Topic: Presentation and Evaluation

1. Session Objective

  • Present the documentary dossier to the group
  • Receive constructive feedback from peers and instructor
  • Prepare dossier integration into the final thesis

2. Presentation Structure (5 min)

1. Topic and research question (30 sec)
   "My thesis focuses on..."

2. Documentary approach (1 min)
   "I consulted ERIC, Google Scholar, and HAL with equations..."

3. Main sources (1 min 30)
   "Three key sources: X shows..., Y nuances..., Z proposes..."

4. Synthesis results (1 min)
   "Points of convergence are... Identified gaps are..."

5. Difficulties and perspectives (1 min)
   "I struggled with... For the next steps, I will..."

3. Presentation Evaluation Rubric

Criterion 0 1 2
Clarity Confusing Understandable Clear and structured
Search quality No strategy Partial strategy Rigorous strategy
Critical thinking Simple description Some critique Nuanced analysis
Time management Too long/short Slightly off Perfect

6. Integration into the Thesis

The documentary dossier feeds directly into: - Chapter 1: Theoretical framework (concepts and definitions) - Chapter 2: Literature review (state of the art) - Chapter 4: Discussion (results vs. literature)

7. Next Steps

  1. Finalize documentary dossier (with integrated feedback)
  2. Develop the thesis theoretical framework
  3. Continue documentary monitoring during writing
  4. Prepare the viva (final bibliography)

Algerian Institutional Context

This course falls within the Algerian regulatory framework for teacher-researcher training:

  • Ministerial decree establishing the Master DLE (Didactics of Foreign Languages) — defines the institutional framework and objectives of the program.
  • National Master DLE curriculum in Algeria — sets the objectives, content and hourly volumes of the Documentary Research module.
  • Decree 712 setting the ENS competition syllabus — determines the tests and programs for ENS entrance competitions.
  • Algerian information literacy framework — national framework defining the documentary competencies expected of Master's students.
  • Benchmark of 10 Algerian universities — comparative study of documentary research teaching practices.
