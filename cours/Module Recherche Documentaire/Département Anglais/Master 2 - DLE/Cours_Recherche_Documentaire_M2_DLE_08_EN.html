Topic: Documentary Synthesis

1. What is a Documentary Synthesis?

A documentary synthesis is an organized text that presents and compares ideas from multiple sources on the same theme. Unlike a simple accumulation of summaries, it constructs new discourse from the sources.

2. Comparison Table

Essential tool for preparing the synthesis:

Criterion Source A Source B Source C

3. Argumentative Moves

Move Connectors Example
Agreement Similarly, likewise, as X notes "As Belacel (2026) shows..."
Opposition However, conversely, in contrast "Unlike X, Y argues that..."
Nuance While... yet, to some extent "While X is right about A, however..."
Complement Moreover, furthermore, additionally "Adding to this perspective..."

4. Structure of a Synthesis

1. INTRODUCTION
   - Theme presentation
   - Plan announcement

2. BODY (2-3 parts)
   - Part 1: Points of convergence
   - Part 2: Divergences and controversies
   - Part 3: Gaps and perspectives

3. CONCLUSION
   - Summary of knowledge
   - Implications for research

5. From Synthesis to Discussion

The documentary synthesis feeds the discussion chapter:

Study results → Comparison with literature
             → Confirmation or refutation of hypotheses
             → New questions raised

Algerian Institutional Context

This course falls within the Algerian regulatory framework for teacher-researcher training:

  • Ministerial decree establishing the Master DLE (Didactics of Foreign Languages) — defines the institutional framework and objectives of the program.
  • National Master DLE curriculum in Algeria — sets the objectives, content and hourly volumes of the Documentary Research module.
  • Decree 712 setting the ENS competition syllabus — determines the tests and programs for ENS entrance competitions.
  • Algerian information literacy framework — national framework defining the documentary competencies expected of Master's students.
  • Benchmark of 10 Algerian universities — comparative study of documentary research teaching practices.
