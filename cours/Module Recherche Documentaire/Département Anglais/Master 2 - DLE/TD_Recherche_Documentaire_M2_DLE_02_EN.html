Tutorial Documentary Research — M2 DLE — Session 02 (EN)

Instructor: BELACEL Madani
Module: Documentary Research
Level: Master 2 — DLE — English Department
Duration: 1h30

Tutorial objective

Move from topic to precise thesis subject.

Exercise 1 — Topic refinement (15 min)

Statement: From « flipped classroom in EFL » propose 3 MA subjects.

Detailed correction:

e.g. flipped classroom and L2 autonomy; videos vs face-to-face; learner perception in secondary school.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 2 — Main question (18 min)

Statement: Write a main question on generative AI and L2 writing.

Detailed correction:

How do trainee teachers scaffold generative AI use for English writing in secondary school?

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 3 — Sub-questions (18 min)

Statement: Derive 3 sub-questions from Ex. 2.

Detailed correction:

AI theoretical frame; teacher practices; learner perception / ethics charter.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 4 — Quality grid (18 min)

Statement: Evaluate 2 questions: clarity, feasibility, MA relevance.

Detailed correction:

Question A too vague; B investigable with defined population.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 5 — Problem paragraph (16 min)

Statement: Write a 10-line problem statement for your topic.

Detailed correction:

Context → situation → gap → research question.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.
