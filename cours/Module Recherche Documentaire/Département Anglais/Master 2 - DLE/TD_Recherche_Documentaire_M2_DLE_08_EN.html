Tutorial Documentary Research — M2 DLE — Session 08 (EN)

Instructor: BELACEL Madani
Module: Documentary Research
Level: Master 2 — DLE — English Department
Duration: 1h30

Tutorial objective

Master APA 7 in-text and references.

Exercise 1 — Direct quote (18 min)

Statement: 2-line direct quote + full APA 7 reference.

Detailed correction:

Quotation marks; (Author, year, p. X); compliant bibliography entry.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 2 — Paraphrase (18 min)

Statement: Paraphrase 4 lines with citation.

Detailed correction:

Original reformulation; no partial copy-paste.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 3 — MLA 9 (15 min)

Statement: Convert 2 APA refs to MLA 9.

Detailed correction:

MLA format; URL/DOI.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 4 — Secondary source (18 min)

Statement: Case: you cite A who cites B — what to do?

Detailed correction:

Cite B (original) if accessible.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 5 — Fix errors (16 min)

Statement: Correct 5 errors in provided bibliography.

Detailed correction:

Title case; DOI; pages; in-text/bib consistency.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

## End of tutorial

Consolidate your documentary protocol: rigorous queries, evaluated sources, structured bibliography.

Documentary protocol reminder

After this session, update your keyword journal (FR/EN pairs), log each Boolean query with database name, exact syntax, hit count, and decision (keep / refine). Export any new reference to Zotero with complete metadata (author, title, journal, DOI). Prepare Session 04 by testing ERIC and JSTOR with the queries validated today.
