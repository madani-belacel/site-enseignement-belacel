Tutorial Documentary Research — M2 DLE — Session 04 (EN)

Instructor: BELACEL Madani
Module: Documentary Research
Level: Master 2 — DLE — English Department
Duration: 1h30

Tutorial objective

Query academic databases.

Exercise 1 — ERIC search (18 min)

Statement: Run your EN query on ERIC; note 3 relevant results.

Detailed correction:

Check journal, abstract, date; note DOI.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 2 — JSTOR (15 min)

Statement: Find 2 JSTOR articles for your topic.

Detailed correction:

Peer-reviewed; export metadata.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 3 — 5 references (18 min)

Statement: Collect 5 refs (3 articles, 1 book, 1 thesis) via Scholar.

Detailed correction:

Table: author, year, title, type, relevance /10.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 4 — Library (18 min)

Statement: Identify 2 subscribed databases useful for MA DLE.

Detailed correction:

Education Source, Springer; remote access if applicable.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 5 — Summary table (16 min)

Statement: Complete table with database used and Zotero link.

Detailed correction:

DOI column; reject commercial sites without author.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

## End of tutorial

Consolidate your documentary protocol: rigorous queries, evaluated sources, structured bibliography.

Documentary protocol reminder

After this session, update your keyword journal (FR/EN pairs), log each Boolean query with database name, exact syntax, hit count, and decision (keep / refine). Export any new reference to Zotero with complete metadata (author, title, journal, DOI). Prepare Session 04 by testing ERIC and JSTOR with the queries validated today.
