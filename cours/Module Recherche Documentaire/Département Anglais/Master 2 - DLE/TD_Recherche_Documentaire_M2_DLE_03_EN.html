Tutorial Documentary Research — M2 DLE — Session 03 (EN)

Instructor: BELACEL Madani
Module: Documentary Research
Level: Master 2 — DLE — English Department
Duration: 1h30

Tutorial objective

Build EN/FR keyword set.

Exercise 1 — Keyword list (15 min)

Statement: List 8 EN + 8 FR keywords for your research question.

Detailed correction:

Example EN set: generative AI, ChatGPT, L2 writing, scaffolding, academic integrity, secondary school, teacher guidance, digital literacy. FR equivalents: intelligence artificielle, production écrite, anglais L2, encadrement, éthique, lycée, didactique, autonomie. Each keyword must map to a concept in your research question.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 2 — Boolean query EN (18 min)

Statement: Write a Scholar query in English.

Detailed correction:

(generative AI OR ChatGPT) AND (writing OR composition) AND (ethics OR integrity) AND (secondary OR high school). Add date filter 2018–2025. Test and record hit count in your query journal.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 3 — Boolean query FR (18 min)

Statement: Write a Cairn query in French.

Detailed correction:

(« intelligence artificielle » OR ChatGPT) AND (anglais OR « langue seconde ») AND didactique AND (lycée OR collège). Verify results on Cairn and HAL; note 3 promising titles.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 4 — Thesaurus (18 min)

Statement: Find 3 ERIC or MeSH descriptors.

Detailed correction:

Artificial Intelligence; Second Language Writing; Academic Integrity. Copy exact descriptor labels from ERIC Thesaurus; use them in an alternative query.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 5 — Refinement (16 min)

Statement: Narrow an overly broad query (8000 hits) to under 200.

Detailed correction:

Add population (secondary EFL), context (Algeria or North Africa if relevant), document type (journal article), and date range. Re-run on ERIC and Scholar; compare hit counts before/after.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

## End of tutorial

Consolidate your documentary protocol: rigorous queries, evaluated sources, structured bibliography.

Documentary protocol reminder

After this session, update your keyword journal (FR/EN pairs), log each Boolean query with database name, exact syntax, hit count, and decision (keep / refine). Export any new reference to Zotero with complete metadata (author, title, journal, DOI). Prepare Session 04 by testing ERIC and JSTOR with the queries validated today.
