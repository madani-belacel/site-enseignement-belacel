Topic: Academic Writing and Citation

1. General Academic Writing Rules

  • Precision: avoid generalizations, define each concept
  • Objectivity: impersonal style (one, it, the author)
  • Structure: clear plan, smooth transitions
  • Citations: support every claim with a source

2. Plagiarism — Definition and Consequences

Plagiarism is copying someone else's work without citing the source.

Forms of plagiarism: - Copy-paste without quotation marks or citation - Too faithful paraphrasing without citation - Translating a text without citing the original - Self-plagiarism: reusing your own work without acknowledgment

Academic consequences: failing grade, degree exclusion, thesis cancellation.

3. How to Cite Correctly

Direct quote (quotation marks):

"Computer-assisted language learning has undergone a profound shift with the arrival of generative AI" (Belacel, 2026, p. 45).

Indirect citation (paraphrase):

According to Belacel (2026), generative AI is transforming language learning practices.

3 P Rule: - Pertinence: the quote serves the argument - Precision: exact page for direct quotes - Prolongement: comment/analyze the quote

4. APA 7th Edition — Basic Format

Journal article:

Author, A. (year). Article title. *Journal Name*, *volume*(issue), pages. DOI

Ex.: Belacel, M. (2026). AI and EFL didactics. Journal of Language Didactics, 12(3), 45-67.

Book:

Author, A. (year). *Book title*. Publisher.

Book chapter:

Author, A. (year). Chapter title. In B. Editor (Ed.), *Book title* (pp. x-y). Publisher.

Web page:

Author, A. (year, date). *Page title*. Site. URL

5. Paraphrasing Exercise

Original:

"Documentary research is an essential skill for any master's student, as it determines the quality of the literature review and, ultimately, the relevance of research findings."

Correct paraphrase:

Mastery of documentary research constitutes a fundamental prerequisite for master's students, insofar as it directly determines the rigor of the literature review and the validity of conclusions (Author, 2026).

  • Decree 712 setting the ENS competition syllabus — determines the tests and programs for ENS entrance competitions.
  • Algerian information literacy framework — national framework defining the documentary competencies expected of Master's students.
  • Benchmark of 10 Algerian universities — comparative study of documentary research teaching practices.
