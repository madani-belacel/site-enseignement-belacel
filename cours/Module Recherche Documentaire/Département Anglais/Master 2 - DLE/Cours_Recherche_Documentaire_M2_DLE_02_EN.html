Research Methodology Course — M2 DLE — Session 02 (EN)

Title: Research Question and Problematic Instructor: BELACEL Madani Module: Research Methodology — Master 2 DLE Duration: 1h30 Academic year: 2025-2026

Theme: Formulating a Research Problematic

From Idea to Research Question

  • The initial idea is often broad and vague → it must be refined, delimited, and transformed into an operational question.
  • Criteria for a good research question: clear, precise, original, relevant, feasible (FINER criteria).

The PICO Method for Language Research

  • P (Population): Who? (e.g., first-year EFL students)
  • I (Intervention): What? (e.g., using a Moodle platform)
  • C (Comparison): Compared to what? (e.g., traditional face-to-face teaching)
  • O (Outcome): What results? (e.g., improved writing skills)

Examples of Research Questions

  • What is the impact of using Moodle on EFL student motivation in the Algerian context?
  • To what extent do interactive online exercises improve English vocabulary acquisition?
  • How do future ENS teachers integrate ICT into their teaching practice?

Algerian Institutional Context

This course is part of the Algerian regulatory framework for teacher training:

  • Ministerial decree creating the Master DLE — institutional framework
  • National Master DLE program in Algeria — objectives and content
  • Algerian information literacy framework — expected competencies
  • Benchmark of 10 Algerian universities — comparative study
