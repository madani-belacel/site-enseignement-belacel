Tutorial Documentary Research — M2 DLE — Session 01 (EN)

Instructor: BELACEL Madani
Module: Documentary Research
Level: Master 2 — DLE — English Department
Duration: 1h30

Tutorial objective

Understand documentary research in the MA thesis.

Exercise 1 — Personal definition (15 min)

Statement: Define documentary research for a language didactics thesis in 5 lines.

Detailed correction:

Process of identifying, selecting, evaluating and synthesising academic sources for a didactic problem.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 2 — Source typology (18 min)

Statement: Classify 8 provided sources by reliability.

Detailed correction:

Meta-analyses > experimental articles > handbook chapters > institutional reports > blogs.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 3 — Thesis diagram (18 min)

Statement: Draw links between introduction, literature review, methodology and discussion.

Detailed correction:

Introduction (state of art) → question → method → results → discussion.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 4 — Personal goals (18 min)

Statement: List 3 documentary skills to acquire by session 10.

Detailed correction:

e.g. Boolean queries, Zotero, CRAP grid, literature review plan.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

Exercise 5 — Timeline (16 min)

Statement: Propose an 8-week timeline (collection, reading, writing).

Detailed correction:

W1–2 collection; W3–4 active reading; W5–6 structuring; W7–8 drafting.

Hints: Work on your personal MA thesis topic; justify each choice in 3–5 sentences.

Expected deliverable: Written response (8–12 lines) filed in your documentary protocol folder.

## End of tutorial

Consolidate your documentary protocol: rigorous queries, evaluated sources, structured bibliography.

Documentary protocol reminder

After this session, update your keyword journal (FR/EN pairs), log each Boolean query with database name, exact syntax, hit count, and decision (keep / refine). Export any new reference to Zotero with complete metadata (author, title, journal, DOI). Prepare Session 04 by testing ERIC and JSTOR with the queries validated today.
