Topic: Preparing the Documentary Dossier

1. What is the Documentary Dossier?

The documentary dossier is an assessed deliverable that brings together all documentary work completed during the semester. It forms the basis of the thesis literature review.

2. Dossier Structure

1. COVER PAGE
   - Title, author, date, supervisor

2. INTRODUCTION (1 page)
   - Research topic and question
   - Documentary approach adopted

3. SEARCH STRATEGY (1-2 pages)
   - Keywords and Boolean equations
   - Databases consulted
   - Selection criteria

4. ANNOTATED BIBLIOGRAPHY (10-15 entries)
   - For each source: APA reference + summary (5-7 lines) + critical evaluation (3-4 lines)

5. DOCUMENTARY SYNTHESIS (3-4 pages)
   - Thematic synthesis comparing sources

6. APPENDICES
   - Detailed reading cards (optional)
   - Zotero export (.rdf)

3. Annotated Bibliography Entry

Structure:

Belacel, M. (2026). Generative AI and writing in EFL.
*Journal of Didactics*, *15*(2), 34-52.

Summary: This study examines ChatGPT's impact on 120 EFL
students' writing. Results show improved fluency but gaps in
lexical accuracy.

Contribution: Useful for AI in didactics theory.
Limitation: French context, not Algerian.

4. Evaluation Criteria

Criterion Weight
Source relevance (10-15 entries, varied, recent) 30%
Quality of summaries and critical evaluations 25%
Documentary synthesis coherence 25%
Formal rigor (APA 7, spelling, structure) 20%

5. Finalization Schedule

Week Task
W-4 Finalize search strategy
W-3 Collect and select 15 sources
W-2 Write reading cards
W-1 Write synthesis + formatting
W0 Final proofreading + submission

6. Final Proofreading Checklist

  • [ ] All sources cited in text
  • [ ] All references in bibliography
  • [ ] Consistent APA 7 format
  • [ ] Spelling and grammar checked
  • [ ] Correct pagination
  • [ ] Zotero file (.rdf) exported

Algerian Institutional Context

This course falls within the Algerian regulatory framework for teacher-researcher training:

  • Ministerial decree establishing the Master DLE (Didactics of Foreign Languages) — defines the institutional framework and objectives of the program.
  • National Master DLE curriculum in Algeria — sets the objectives, content and hourly volumes of the Documentary Research module.
  • Decree 712 setting the ENS competition syllabus — determines the tests and programs for ENS entrance competitions.
  • Algerian information literacy framework — national framework defining the documentary competencies expected of Master's students.
  • Benchmark of 10 Algerian universities — comparative study of documentary research teaching practices.
